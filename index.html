About

A trainee community for computational social science

Computational approaches to understanding societies and cultures span disciplines including psychology, linguistics, sociology, education, and computer science. While many Stanford researchers are leaders in computational social science, there is no central department or program where trainees can discover related work, exchange ideas, and build connections outside their immediate labs.

The Cultures, Collectives, and Computation (C³) Reading Group exists to fill that gap. Following a by trainees, for trainees journal club model, we host biweekly discussions among graduate students, postdocs, research assistants, and other early-career researchers across Stanford. Our sessions are an opportunity to engage with cutting-edge research, present early-stage work, and develop skills in critical engagement and interdisciplinary communication.
